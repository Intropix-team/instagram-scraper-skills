# Privacy policy: Intropix Instagram scraper skills

Last updated: 21-10-2026. Contact: team.intropix@gmail.com.

**What the skills are.** Instructions and one helper script that let an AI
agent run the Intropix Instagram scrapers on Apify on your behalf. The skills
themselves run on your machine or in your agent; Intropix operates no server
for them and receives nothing from them directly.

**What is sent, and to whom.**

- To **Apify** (Apify Technologies s.r.o.), through Apify's MCP server or the
  Apify CLI that you connect with your own Apify account: the Instagram
  usernames or post links you ask about and the scraper settings. Apify's own
  privacy policy applies: https://apify.com/privacy-policy
- The scraper run is operated by **Intropix** inside your Apify account. What
  it processes, on what legal basis and for how long is described in the
  privacy notice on each scraper's page:
  - https://apify.com/intropix/instagram-stories-scraper
  - https://apify.com/intropix/instagram-posts-reels-scraper
  - https://apify.com/intropix/instagram-posts-reels-scraper-pro
- To **Instagram's CDN**, when a skill downloads media files: ordinary HTTPS
  requests for the media links the scraper returned.

**What the skills store.** Only the files and JSON/CSV records they write to
the folder you work in. Intropix never receives a copy. You decide how long to
keep them; the scrapers' terms ask you to keep results no longer than your
business purpose needs (as a guideline, 30 days) and never to use them for
personal accounts.

**Children.** The skills are not intended for people under 18 and must not be
used to collect data about persons under 16.

**Your rights.** For data the scrapers processed, see the privacy notices
above. Questions or exclusion requests: team.intropix@gmail.com.
